Share your iPhone screen

Start a screen broadcast, then keep this Safari page open. Enter the same room code and relay URL on the Nintendo Switch receiver.

Cast a video or website URL

Paste a direct video link (MP4 or compatible HLS) or a web address. The Switch will open the link in its receiver player.

Ready · Screen sharing requires Safari permission and a deployed relay.

URL casting sends a link only; the Switch must be able to open and play it. DRM-protected services and many ordinary webpages cannot be cast this way. This does not mirror your iPhone screen.
